Open Upāya Skill Share

The skill share runs on claude.ai. Sign in there to join; if the page doesn't open for you, contact Rye Libre to be added.

How it works

Join

List your skills, languages and the places you know, and make the 2-hour pledge.

Ask

Request 2 hours from anyone for advice or hands-on help. Say what you need.

Give

I will give 2 hours to members who ask, when I can. Each profile shows how often its owner responds with help.

Vouch

For members you have worked with or after working, vouch for the skills to build network trust.
